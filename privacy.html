Privacy Policy

Whistling Coach · last updated 15 August 2026

The short version

Whistling Coach collects nothing, stores nothing about you, and sends nothing anywhere. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no network connection to any server of ours — because there is no server of ours.

Your microphone

Whistling Coach listens to your whistling to measure its pitch. That is the entire purpose of the app.

The audio is analysed on your device, moment by moment, and then discarded. It is never written to storage and never transmitted. Whistling Coach has no recording feature, and the app contains no code that could save or send audio.

While the microphone is live, Whistling Coach shows a “listening” indicator on screen, in addition to the orange dot iOS displays.

What is stored on your device

A small settings file, held only on your device, containing:

  • the song, mode and difficulty you last used
  • your pitch adjustment, in semitones
  • a learned estimate of which octave you tend to whistle in
  • the name you chose for Party mode
  • whether you have seen the introduction

You can erase all of it at any time by deleting the app. None of it is transmitted, backed up to us, or accessible to anyone but you.

Party mode

Party mode lets several people play together on the same Wi-Fi, or over Bluetooth when there is no network. Devices talk directly to each other. There is no server, and nothing leaves the local network.

What is exchanged between devices:

  • the display name you typed
  • the room code
  • whose turn it is, and the song and settings the host chose
  • your live pitch, as a number in cents, so others can watch the trace
  • notes hit, how close they were, and the scores

No audio is transmitted. The largest message the app can send is the list of players.

Children

Whistling Coach is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Third parties

There are none. Whistling Coach includes no third-party SDKs, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. It does not use the Advertising Identifier.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published here and the date at the top will change.
